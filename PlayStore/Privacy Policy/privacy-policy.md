# Privacy Policy for Death Ink

**Effective date:** 25 July 2026
**Last updated:** 25 July 2026

Death Ink ("the app", "we", "us"), developed by Santhosh, respects your privacy.
This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and your choices.
By using Death Ink you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Death Ink is fully playable without signing in, as an anonymous guest. Some data
is collected only if you choose to sign in with Google to join the leaderboard.

Collected automatically (all players):
- Anonymous account identifier (Firebase UID): a random ID to save your progress
  and scores. Not linked to your real identity.
- Push notification token (FCM): a device token used to deliver in-game
  notifications.
- Gameplay data: level progress, best times, death counts and star ratings.

Collected only if you sign in with Google (optional):
- Email address: provided by Google Sign-In, used to identify your account.
- Display name: a nickname you type, shown on the public leaderboard.
- Country: a country you select, shown on the public leaderboard.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, files, phone
number, or any payment information. The app contains no ads and no in-app
purchases.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To save and sync your game progress and scores across sessions.
- To display the global leaderboard (display name + country + score).
- To send you in-game notifications.
- To operate, maintain and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

## 3. Service Providers

Death Ink uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging)
and Google Sign-In to provide accounts, cloud storage of scores, and
notifications. Your data is processed on Google's servers under Google's terms:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy
Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your cloud data is kept until you delete it. You can permanently delete your
account and all associated data at any time from inside the app: Profile screen,
then "Delete account". You may also contact us to request deletion.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Death Ink is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. Contact us and we will delete it.

## 6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data via
the in-app deletion feature or by contacting us at the email below.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new
"Last updated" date.
