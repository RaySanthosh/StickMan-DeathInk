Death is not the end. It's the whole point.

Death Ink is a hand-drawn, precision stickman platformer set inside a cursed notebook. Every time you die, your name is written on its pages — and a sarcastic god of death is always watching, always laughing.

Run, jump, and dash through 10 deadly chapters packed with spikes, firing darts, collapsing floors, vanishing platforms and traps that only reveal themselves the hard way. One mistake and it's over. But you always come back — reborn on the same page, a little angrier, a little sharper.

FEATURES
- 10 hand-drawn chapters that get meaner as you go
- Tight, responsive one-more-try platforming
- Lethal traps: spikes, darts, crushers, disappearing floors and more
- A merciless narrator who mocks every single death
- Star ratings + best-time and fewest-deaths challenges
- Global leaderboard — climb it and rule the Note
- Play offline as a guest; sign in only if you want your name on the board
- No ads. No in-app purchases. Just you versus the Note.

Can you clear every chapter... or will the notebook fill up with your name first?

The Shinigami are waiting. Don't keep them entertained for too long.
